Final test on 2025 to 2026
Every setting was frozen before this ran. Elo v3 was fit on 2022 to 2023 with k_region and
Platt scaling from 2024. The draft model was fit on 2022 to 2023 and chosen on 2024.
draft model  Elo + champion win rate + player on champion  {'pc_shrink': 1, 'window': 60, 'champ_shrink': 160}

How clean is this? 2025 to 2026 had already been looked at twice (v1 and the rejected v2)
before v3 was designed. v3 was never tuned on these years, but knowing that v1 was
overconfident there shaped the design, so treat this as nearly clean, not pristine.
Elo v1 itself was scored on these games before, with the same params.json.
